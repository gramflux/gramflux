# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Critique code and evidence, not people.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and publishing others' private information
are unacceptable. Respect account privacy and Telegram's terms.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or remove a
contributor after considering severity and context. Report conduct concerns privately
to the hosting project's maintainers once a private contact is configured. Do not
publish identifying details about affected people. No dedicated conduct contact is
configured for this unpublished engineering alpha.
